Common questions

How do I start?

Open the official PayEase Mini App from Telegram and complete the registration steps.

How does KYC work?

Submit the required information and documents. A licensed partner reviews the application and may request clarification.

Who decides my loan?

The licensed financial partner makes the final credit decision. PayEase shows the applicable fees, net amount and repayment before signing.

What should I never share in chat?

Never share passwords, verification codes, full card numbers or identity documents with unofficial accounts.
